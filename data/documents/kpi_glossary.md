# Revenue measures

Gross sales is the gross order value for non-cancelled certified orders. Net sales subtracts discounts and recognized refunds. Net revenue adds tax and shipping to net sales. Numerical values must be obtained from the certified Gold tables, not calculated from this glossary.

# Customer measures

Average order value is net sales divided by distinct certified orders. Conversion rate is distinct sessions with a purchase divided by distinct eligible sessions. Both definitions use zero when their denominator is zero.

# Operations measures

Return rate is returned units divided by sold units. Stockout rate is product-store observations at zero on-hand divided by all inventory observations. The governed KPI catalog is the authoritative mapping to certified tables and measure columns.
