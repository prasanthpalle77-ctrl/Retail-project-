# Standard return window

Customers may return an unopened, unused item within 30 calendar days of delivery. The original order number is required. Final-sale items and gift cards are not eligible for return.

# Refund handling

After the warehouse accepts and inspects the item, the refund is issued to the original payment method. Normal processing takes five to seven business days. Shipping charges are refunded only when the item was damaged, defective, or shipped incorrectly.

# Damaged items

Damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with the order number and photographs of the item and packaging. Customer support creates the replacement or refund case.
